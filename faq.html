HELP / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions, answered

Are these products for human use?

No. Products are listed for laboratory research only and are not for human or veterinary use.

Where do I find the COA for a lot?

Search the COA library by lot number. If a certificate is not listed, contact the supplier for documentation before relying on a result.

Why are some prices shown as “From”?

A starting price appears in the catalog, but size-specific variants are not confirmed. Those products cannot be added to the cart at a guessed price.

Is purity testing available?

Only a published, lot-specific certificate can support a testing result. The quality page lists documented methods when available.

How should I store a product?

Follow its verified specifications and lot documentation. Storage instructions are not listed when no verified handling guidance is available.

How quickly will an order ship?

Current dispatch times are not published yet. Check the shipping page or contact support before placing an order.

Can these products be returned?

See the returns page for published terms. An order policy has not yet been supplied.
