Understanding Glow Aminos Product Names

Glow Aminos uses FG-series names for selected research compounds. These labels help distinguish products within the catalog while keeping each compound tied to its individual specifications and batch documentation.

01

FG1-S

FG1-S is the Glow Aminos product label for semaglutide research material.

02

FG2-T identifies tirzepatide within the Glow Aminos research catalog.

03

FG3-R

FG3-R is the catalog name used for retatrutide research material.
