Are the products for human or veterinary use?

No. They are listed for laboratory research use only.

Where do I find a certificate?

Use the lot lookup above or visit the COA library. If no certificate is listed, do not assume a test result.

Why do some prices start with “From”?

They show the lowest listed price. Product variants have not been added without confirmed size and price information.

Can I pay on this site?

Checkout will accept payments only after a secure payment provider has been connected and verified.
