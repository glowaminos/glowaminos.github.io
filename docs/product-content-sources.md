# Product detail research and publication boundaries

Reviewed September 24, 2026. This is a source trail for original, concise Glow Aminos catalog summaries. It is not evidence that Glow Aminos sells the same formulations, lots, vial sizes, or tested materials as either reference merchant.

## Reference sites

- Amino Club's [BPC-157 profile](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/bpc-157) gives the peptide length, sequence, CAS number, formula, and compound molecular weight.
- Its [GHK-Cu profile](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/ghk-cu) identifies the copper complex, peptide sequence, and CAS number.
- Its [MOTS-c profile](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/mots-c) gives the 16-residue mitochondrial-derived peptide sequence.
- Its [NAD+ profile](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/nad-plus) gives the coenzyme class, CAS number, formula, and molecular weight.
- Its [GLOW](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/glow) and [KLOW](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/products/klow) profiles show how *that merchant* uses those blend names. Glow Aminos blend composition is **not confirmed** by those pages.
- Its [store catalog](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/store) identifies other similarly named research compounds and distinguishes Melanotan I from Melanotan II.
- The [Amino Club tesamorelin COA](https://www.aminoclub.com/coa/Biogenica_CoA_Tesamorelin_10MG_TES0001.pdf) contains compound identifiers used as *general identity information only*, never as Glow Aminos lot results.
- [Refined Biolabs catalog](https://refinedbiolabs.com/) and [COA library](https://refinedbiolabs.com/coa) were checked as requested. Its COA library currently displays no published certificates. Any product purity or batch statements on its pages belong to that merchant.
- [Retatrutide trial publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37385280/) documents the GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor activity of the *named molecule*, not this product.

## Shipping and returns

[Amino Club shipping](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/shipping) and [returns](https://www.aminoclub.com/us/returns), and [Refined Biolabs shipping](https://refinedbiolabs.com/shipping) and [returns](https://refinedbiolabs.com/returns), were reviewed for page structure. Their shipping cutoffs, free shipping offers, carrier terms, replacement promises, eligibility windows, and refund terms are **not** Glow Aminos terms. Product pages link to Glow Aminos's existing shipping and returns pages, which state that these details require merchant confirmation.

## Before publishing specific product claims

For each Glow Aminos item obtain its exact vial label, stated strength, formulation, compound identity and subtype, storage guidance, and a batch-specific COA. Compare the COA product and lot number to the stock that ships. Verify the actual ingredient ratios for named blends, the subtype of Melanotan, and the definition of TB-500. Add purity, testing methods, pass results, lab names, and lot numbers only from that documentation. Obtain merchant-approved shipping and returns policies before presenting fixed rates, delivery promises, or refund eligibility.
